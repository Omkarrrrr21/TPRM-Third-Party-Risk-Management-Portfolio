# Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) Portfolio

## Mayekar Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

A professional, practical Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) portfolio demonstrating the complete lifecycle of managing information-security and technology risks associated with vendors, suppliers, service providers, and other third parties.

---

## Project Overview

This portfolio demonstrates how an organization can establish, operate, monitor, assess, treat, and govern third-party risks throughout the vendor lifecycle.

The project covers:

- Vendor governance
- Vendor onboarding
- Due diligence
- Risk assessment
- Security assessment
- Contract and SLA security requirements
- Incident and breach management
- Business continuity and disaster recovery
- Privacy and data protection
- Continuous monitoring
- Periodic and trigger-based reassessment
- Risk treatment and remediation
- Risk acceptance and escalation
- Vendor offboarding and exit management
- Evidence management
- Management reporting and governance
